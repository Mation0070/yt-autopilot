## What it will avoid
- Generic scandal titles lacking named figures or brands (e.g., 'Exposed: The Oil Bribery Scam', 'Global Oil Machine')
- Broad macro-economic evolution videos without a central protagonist
- Slow-paced long-form intros that result in single-digit retention percentages

## New rules for scripts & titles
- Feature recognizable names or major brands in the title and thumbnail (e.g., Motsepe, Obasanjo, Ethiopian Airlines) instead of generic terms like 'Oil Machine' or 'Bribery Scam'.
- Use specific dollar amounts ($3.7B, $11B) and clear conflict frames ('Strive Masiyiwa vs Robert Mugabe') in Short hooks to push retention past 100%.
- Ensure long-form video hooks immediately present the core story within the first 15 seconds to prevent steep early drop-off.
- Use high-performing Short topics as proof-of-concept tests before committing resources to long-form deep dives.
- Avoid standalone 'Exposed:' titles unless paired with a household brand name or well-known public figure.
- Maintain tight pacing in the first 60 seconds of long-form videos, as videos dropping below 10% retention suffer from weak intro hooks.
