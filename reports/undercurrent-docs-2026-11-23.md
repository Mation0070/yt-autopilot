## What the AI will do more of
- US-China semiconductor export bans and equipment monopolies (ASML, TSMC, Nvidia)
- Critical mineral supply chain bottlenecks and rare earth mineral wars
- Strategic maritime chokepoints (Strait of Malacca, Suwalki Gap, Red Sea shipping routes)
- Defense industrial base vulnerabilities (submarine shipbuilding, missile stockpiles, defense contractor monopolies)

## What it will avoid
- Generic corporate origin stories or luxury brand histories (e.g., LVMH, Diamond monopolies)
- Dry academic titles formatted like Wikipedia entries (e.g., 'Palantir: Data Integration and Modern Defense Technology')
- Long-form videos with unoptimized, descriptive-only titles lacking dynamic hooks
- General retail/consumer wholesale topics without national security/geopolitical stakes

## New rules for scripts & titles
- Use dollar values paired with conflict framing in titles (e.g., '$25 Billion Aviation Gamble' or 'China's Rare Earth Trap').
- Focus content heavily on defense technology, semiconductor geopolitics, and trade chokepoints.
- Keep Short titles concise and punchy; incorporate curiosity gaps using words like 'Trap', 'Gamble', 'Chokepoint', and 'Gatekeeper'.
- Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds with high-stakes geopolitical implications rather than historical context.
- Repurpose successful Short concepts into long-form videos rather than publishing raw long-form documentaries cold.
- Maintain high retention on Shorts (>70% view duration) by pacing script transitions around specific structural bottlenecks or military implications.
