## What it will avoid
- Abstract or textbook title formulations lacking clear tension (e.g., 'Data Integration and Modern Defense Technology')
- Vague topic hooks without clear brand or geography anchors (e.g., 'The Hidden Tax Inside Every Smartphone')
- Pure long-form uploads without an accompanying Short snippet to drive initial awareness

## New rules for scripts & titles
- Anchor titles with specific, eye-popping dollar figures ($25 Billion, $61B, $5 Trillion) or iconic industrial locations (iPhone City).
- Inject narrative tension into titles (e.g., 'Disrupting the Pentagon', 'Gamble', 'War Deterrent') rather than textbook descriptions.
- Avoid dry, corporate resume titles like 'Palantir: Data Integration and Modern Defense Technology' or 'Arm Holdings: The Architect...'.
- Structure Short hooks around high-stakes curiosity gaps to push retention above 80% (as achieved by the Taiwan Silicon Shield and LVMH shorts).
- Keep Shorts tightly edited to encourage re-watching, leveraging looping dynamics that drove LVMH's 141% retention rate.
- Use high-performing Short topics as direct smoke-tests before scripting full-length documentaries.
