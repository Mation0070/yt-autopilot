## What the AI will do more of
- Semiconductor supply chain battles (e.g., ASML lithography monopolies, TSMC mega-fab bets).
- Defense mega-projects and military resource chokepoints (e.g., rare earth monopolies, military aviation overspending).
- Strategic maritime and infrastructure bottlenecks (e.g., Strait of Hormuz oil routes, subsea internet cable security).

## What it will avoid
- Consumer luxury, retail, and commodities stories without geopolitical conflict (e.g., LVMH empire, diamond monopoly, Yiwu wholesale market).
- Dry, descriptive title formats lacking strong narrative tension or clear stakes.
- Releasing long-form content without repurposing proven Short hooks into compelling long-form intros.

## New rules for scripts & titles
- Lead Short titles with specific dollar amounts ($B/$T) combined with high-stakes tension words ('Gamble', 'Trap', 'Chokepoint').
- Focus topic selection on defense technology, geopolitical bottlenecks, and semiconductor wars over standard corporate histories.
- Aim for 45-second Shorts if visual pacing can sustain >70% retention, as these trigger YouTube's feed far better than 25-second clips.
- Eliminate dry, academic titles like 'Data Integration and Modern Defense Technology' in favor of dramatic story hooks.
- Front-load the first 10 seconds of long-form videos with intense narrative stakes to fix low early retention (currently averaging under 38%).
- Acknowledge data noise: rely on view-through rate and retention metrics rather than raw sub count when testing new sub-niches.
