## What the AI will do more of
- High-dollar aviation, chip war, and defense supply chain mini-docs
- Inside secret mega-factories and supply hubs (e.g., TSMC mega-fabs, defense shipyards)
- Geopolitical tech struggles (e.g., subsea internet cables, rare earth monopolies)
- Controversial corporate disruptions and mega-mergers with high financial stakes

## What it will avoid
- Abstract titles lacking concrete numbers, brand names, or urgent conflicts
- Relying solely on long-form formats for growth before establishing a core Shorts audience
- Slow-burn script hooks that yield sub-30% retention in the first 15 seconds

## New rules for scripts & titles
- Lead titles with specific, eye-catching dollar values ($25 Billion, $61B, $5 Trillion) to create instant intrigue.
- Focus topics on high-stakes industrial power, defense technology, supply chain control, and corporate monopolies.
- Structure Shorts scripts for continuous loops or rapid payoff to push retention past 70-100%.
- Use strong entity names (Foxconn, Taiwan, Pentagon, SpaceX) early in the video and title rather than abstract concepts.
- Avoid passive titles like 'The Hidden Tax' or vague tech summaries; frame stories around conflict, disruption, or gambles.
- Keep using Shorts to feed long-form topic validation until subscriber counts provide a baseline for long-form impressions.
